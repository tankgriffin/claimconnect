# ClaimConnect - Personal Injury Lawyer Lead Generation Website
## Product Requirements Document

## 1. Project Overview

### Brand Identity
- **Name**: ClaimConnect
- **Color Palette**: Blue and white (trust-building professional palette)
- **Target Market**: Australia-wide personal injury and compensation law firms

### Vision
Create a high-converting lead generation website that connects injured individuals with qualified personal injury and compensation lawyers across Australia through a streamlined, trust-building experience.

### Goals
- Generate highly qualified leads for personal injury law firms
- Improve lead quality through multi-step qualification process
- Maximize conversion through optimized user experience
- Establish trust and credibility with potential clients
- Provide clear pathways for different injury types

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Users
- Individuals who have suffered personal injuries
- People seeking compensation for workplace injuries
- Motor vehicle accident victims
- Medical malpractice victims
- Slip and fall accident victims

### User Characteristics
- Age: 25-65 years old
- Often stressed, confused, or overwhelmed
- May have limited legal knowledge
- Seeking immediate help and guidance
- Mobile-first users (60%+ mobile traffic expected)
- Value trust signals and professional appearance

## 4. Core Features & Functionality

### 4.1 Hero Section with Service Categories
**Design Pattern**: White boxes with blue accent icons (inspired by SmartFinancial)

**Injury Categories to Include**:
- Motor Vehicle Accidents
- Workplace Injuries
- Medical Malpractice
- Slip & Fall Accidents
- Product Liability
- Public Liability
- Workers' Compensation
- Assault Claims

**Each Category Box Should Have**:
- Clear, recognizable blue icon
- Category title in bold
- Brief description (1-2 lines)
- "Get Help Now" CTA button
- Hover effects with blue accent

### 4.2 Multi-Step Lead Qualification Form

#### Step 1: Injury Type Selection
**Purpose**: Route to the right lawyers & set context
- "What type of injury or incident brings you here today?"
- Visual grid with icons matching hero section categories
- Progress indicator (Step 1 of 5)
- Large, clickable cards with icons

#### Step 2: Incident Details
**Purpose**: Qualify severity and timeframe
- "When did this incident occur?" (date picker)
- "Have you sought medical attention?" (Yes/No radio buttons)
- "Are you currently receiving treatment?" (Yes/No radio buttons)
- "Have you been unable to work due to this injury?" (Yes/No/Partially radio buttons)
- Progress indicator (Step 2 of 5)

#### Step 3: Current Situation
**Purpose**: Understand urgency and legal status
- "Have you spoken to a lawyer about this incident?" (Yes/No/Currently looking radio buttons)
- "Has anyone admitted fault or responsibility?" (Yes/No/Unsure radio buttons)
- "Do you have insurance that might cover this?" (Yes/No/Unsure radio buttons)
- Progress indicator (Step 3 of 5)

#### Step 4: Personal Information
**Purpose**: Contact details for qualified leads only
- Full Name (required text field)
- Phone Number (required with validation)
- Email Address (required with validation)
- State/Territory (required dropdown)
- Preferred contact time (Morning/Afternoon/Evening checkboxes)
- Progress indicator (Step 4 of 5)

#### Step 5: Brief Description & Consent
**Purpose**: Final qualification and context for lawyers
- "Please briefly describe what happened" (text area, optional)
- Privacy policy consent (required checkbox)
- Marketing consent (optional checkbox)
- "Submit My Claim" button
- Progress indicator (Step 5 of 5)

## 14. Design Specifications

### 14.1 Typography
- **Primary Font**: Professional sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Open Sans)
- **Heading Hierarchy**: H1 (32px), H2 (28px), H3 (24px), H4 (20px)
- **Body Text**: 16px minimum for readability
- **Line Height**: 1.5 for body text, 1.2 for headings

### 14.2 Layout & Spacing
- **Max Content Width**: 1200px
- **Grid System**: 12-column responsive grid
- **Margins**: 16px mobile, 24px tablet, 32px desktop
- **Card Spacing**: 24px between elements
- **Section Padding**: 60px desktop, 40px mobile

### 14.3 Interactive Elements
- **Buttons**: Rounded corners (8px), blue background, white text
- **Form Fields**: Border radius 4px, blue focus states
- **Cards**: Subtle shadows, hover effects with blue accent
- **Progress Bar**: Blue fill with step indicators
